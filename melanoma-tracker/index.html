Melanoma Phase 3 Readout & FDA Approval Tracker

A curated tracker that treats Phase 3 melanoma readouts as structured signals, not one-off news. Each positive readout is a leading indicator of a future FDA filing — so the tool follows drugs across two linked layers: the data readout, then the regulatory journey (filing → PDUFA → decision).

Built after Merck & Moderna's INTerpath-001 positive readout (19 Aug 2026) — the first Phase 3 win for an mRNA individualised neoantigen therapy — to capture events like it systematically.

Methodology
How this tracker is built

📡 Curated, not scraped

Readout timing and outcomes are announced by companies via press releases, earnings calls, and medical meetings — they are not reliably held in ClinicalTrials.gov. So each entry is captured from a public announcement, by hand.

🔗 Enriched with trial data

Every readout is linked to its structured trial record — NCT ID, design, setting, primary endpoint — sourced from ClinicalTrials.gov, so the leading signal sits next to the underlying trial.

✔️ Sourced & flagged

Every entry cites its source. Nothing is invented — no hazard ratios or dates are guessed. Where a field can't be verified from public disclosure, it is flagged rather than filled in.

Why two layers? A positive Phase 3 readout is a leading indicator; the FDA decision that follows is a lagging one. Tracking both — connected through the drug — turns scattered news into a visible pipeline: which positive readouts have converted to filings, which are approaching a PDUFA date, and which have crossed the line. Failures are tracked too, because a negative readout is just as much a signal as a positive one.
